Background

The Origin of Bombsight

"You were everyone's favorite from the start. Especially Clara. Everything they wanted us to be, everything they were working towards, they saw it in you. I fucking hated you for it."

— Bombsight to Soldier Boy

Robbie, better known as Bombsight, is an upcoming main character of the Amazon series Vought Rising, a prequel spin-off series of The Boys. He also appears as a minor character in Season Five of The Boys.

Very little is known about Robbie, except that he served as a combat pilot in the U.S. Armed Forces. Sometime later, he was given V-One by Frederick Vought himself, subsequently transforming Robbie into a successful Supe known as Bombsight and granting him powerful superhuman abilities.

At some point, Bombsight met and fell in love with Clara Vought, but she chose Soldier Boy instead. During the 1950s, Bombsight was featured in commercials for Vought Brand Weight Control Tablets. In 1953, he starred in a film called The Curse of Fu Manchu.

Personality

What Drives Him

Unlike most other Supes, Bombsight is not driven by greed, lust, ego or power. He has a deep desire to find someone special and not spend eternity alone. Because of this, he deeply despises his immortality and jumped at the offer Soldier Boy made him to become human again in exchange for the V-One, smiling with joy when he lost his powers.

Bombsight is very stubborn, choosing to abandon Golden Geisha waiting for years so she could change her mind about the V-One despite telling him she would never accept immortality. Nevertheless, even after spending years separated he still loves her, immediately arriving to save Geisha who was being held as a hostage by The Boys and Sister Sage.

Bombsight has admitted he hates Soldier Boy, because back in their early days he was the one who always got the attention, love and admiration of everyone. Despite this, he is capable of sympathizing with his former teammate because he could not be with the person he loved most, Clara.

Former Powers

Flight

Bombsight can levitate and propel himself through the air at rapid speeds. Unlike other flying Supes, he lands with extreme force that craters the ground like a bomb.

∞️

Immortality

One of the first Supes injected with V-One, the immortality-granting serum that halts biological aging and preserves youth indefinitely.

💪

Superhuman Strength

Punched 80 years of rust off a safe containing V-One. Recognized as one of a dozen Supes who could punch with such force. Ragdolled both Butcher and Kimiko.

🛡️

Nigh-Invulnerability

Described as effectively unbreakable. Completely bulletproof and durable enough to endure repeated direct blows from Soldier Boy with minimal injury.

👂

Superhuman Reflexes

Dodged Soldier Boy's punches multiple times and retaliated effectively, demonstrating combat reflexes far beyond human capability.

👂

Superhuman Hearing

Like all Supes, Bombsight can hear higher frequencies that humans cannot, granting him enhanced auditory perception.

Trivia

Did You Know?

The casting call for this character used the name Flyboy.

Bombsight's superhero name is derived from the military bombsight device, a tool designed to accurately target and deploy aerial bombs.

His military background as a fighter pilot in the US Air Force is likely a nod to Green Lantern (Hal Jordan) and Captain Marvel (Carol Danvers), both of whom have the ability to fly.
